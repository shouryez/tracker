DsaKaro times every LeetCode and GeeksforGeeks problem you open, catches every verdict, and syncs it all to your free DsaKaro dashboard, so you always know what you've solved, how long it really took, and what to revise before you forget it.

WHAT IT DOES

• Times every problem automatically
Open a problem and a small timer starts on its own. Switch tabs, step away or stop typing for three minutes and it pauses itself, so your hours are honest. You can also pause and resume by hand.

• Catches every verdict
Accepted, Wrong Answer, Time Limit Exceeded and more are recorded the moment the judge answers, with language, runtime and memory. No copying links, no spreadsheets.

• One honest question after Accepted
Did you solve it on your own, with a hint, or after seeing the solution? Pick the pattern that cracked it and write a one-line takeaway. It takes ten seconds and makes your revision queue far more accurate.

• Your memory of the problem, on the page
Open the panel to see when you last solved it, your best time, how you solved it, and your own takeaway from last time. Stuck? Reveal gentle hints one at a time instead of jumping to the editorial.

• A checklist of your own mistakes
After a wrong answer, a short pre-submit checklist appears, starting with the slips you've made before (edge cases, overflow, off-by-one).

• Synced with Striver's sheet on takeUforward
Your Striver A2Z and SDE sheets stay identical on both sites: tick a problem on takeuforward.org and it's ticked on DsaKaro, tick it on DsaKaro and it's ticked on takeuforward.org. Open the sheet there once and all your past ticks line up.

• Your past history, imported
When you connect, your existing LeetCode submissions are imported from your own logged-in session, so your dashboard starts full, not empty.

• Today at a glance
The toolbar popup shows problems solved, sessions and focus time today, and lets you save any page (an article, a video at its timestamp, a problem) to come back to later.

• Reminders and focus mode
Optional reminders at your usual solving hour and before contests. During a focus session started on DsaKaro, time on coding sites counts as focus and everything else as away.

YOUR DASHBOARD
Everything lands on dsakaro.site: a heatmap of your solves, streaks, a spaced-repetition revision queue (1, 3, 7, 16, 35 and 90 days), your Striver, NeetCode and Blind 75 sheets ticked automatically, a daily to-do list, and study groups with friends. Free for students.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Only the code you submit on LeetCode is saved, for bug autopsy on failed submissions. Delete it any time in Settings.
• Your passwords and LeetCode / GFG cookies never leave your browser.
• Focus mode keeps only two totals (focus and away time), never the sites you visit.
• No ads, no selling data.
Full policy: https://dsakaro.site/privacy

HOW TO START
1. Install the extension.
2. Click the DsaKaro icon and press Connect (sign in with Google on dsakaro.site).
3. Open any LeetCode or GeeksforGeeks problem and start solving. That's it.
